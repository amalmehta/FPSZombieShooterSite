# FPS Zombie Shooter

A first-person 3D zombie survival shooter: hold a ruined city plaza at night against waves of walkers, runners and brutes. Plays in your browser or as a Mac app.

![Gameplay: zombies coming down a lamp-lit street at night](preview.jpg)

**Links**

- [Play it in your browser](https://amalmehta.github.io/FPSZombieShooterSite/) (keyboard and mouse)
- [Download the Mac app](https://github.com/amalmehta/FPSZombieShooterSite/releases/latest) (Apple silicon and Intel disk images)

This repository holds only the built website. It's published automatically from the game's source repository, so changes made here directly are overwritten.
